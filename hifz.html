A full-time program for students ready to devote their day to memorizing the Qur'an — planned, but not yet open.

This program has not started yet

What the Program Will Cover

A full day given to the Qur'an, with the same three pillars behind every one of our programs.

New Memorization

الحفظ الجديد

Daily memorization targets set to each student's capacity, heard and corrected by the teacher every single day.

Systematic Revision

المراجعة

Recent revision of newly memorized portions alongside a long-term cycle through previous ajzā', so nothing is lost as the student advances.

Tajweed & Character

التجويد والأخلاق

The rules of recitation applied directly to what is being memorized, together with a daily session on manners and Prophetic guidance.
