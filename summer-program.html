What Your Child Will Gain

Qur'an Memorization

الحفظ
  • New memorization: tailored daily goals based on each student's capability.
  • Recent revision (qarībah): daily reinforcement of newly memorized portions.
  • Long-term revision (ba'īdah): a systematic cycle through previous ajzā' to keep everything strong.

Recitation & Tajweed

التلاوة والتجويد
  • Individual guidance: one-on-one listening to build fluency and remove fear of reading.
  • Daily micro-lessons: fundamental rules such as Nūn Sākinah, Madd, and Makhārij.
  • Love of recitation: instilling appreciation for the melodic beauty of the Qur'an.

Character & Fun

الأخلاق
  • Daily ethics session: manners, Prophetic stories, and a daily Hadith focus.
  • Games and outdoor time during a full midday recess.
  • Congregational prayer: students pray Dhuhr together each day.

How Registration Works

Every family gets personal attention before the program begins.

  1. Fill out the registration form. Tell us about your child, their Qur'an background, and whether you'd like the 4-week or 8-week option.
  2. Receive a personal call from Shaykh Muhammad. He will call you directly to answer any questions you may have.
  3. Attend the open house. All registered parents are invited before the program starts.
  4. Individual assessment. Each child is personally assessed by the Shaykh to ensure they are set up for success.
  5. Pay securely online. Fees are paid by card through Stripe.
