About Us

Dar Al Quran Wal Hikmah — the Home of the Qur'an and Wisdom — is a place where children and adults strengthen their connection with the Book of Allah, together as a community.

Shaykh Muhammad Abdelaziz

Shaykh Muhammad Abdelaziz leads all of our programs personally — from teaching the classical rules of Tajweed to hearing each student's recitation one-on-one and assessing every child who joins the summer program.

A full biography of the Shaykh — his studies, teachers, and ijāzāt — is coming soon, in shā' Allāh.

Our Philosophy: Ethics & Akhlāq

“Verily, I have only been sent to perfect righteous character.”

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — Musnad Aḥmad

We believe that Qur'anic education is incomplete without character development. Alongside memorization and recitation, our sessions highlight good manners, honesty, patience, and resolving conflicts with mercy — instilling practical ethics for daily life. Our aim is to nurture students, together as a community, to be living examples of the Qur'an.
