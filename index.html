Our Three Pillars

Every program we offer is built on the same foundation.

Qur'an Mastery

إتقان القرآن

Structured pathways for new memorization, retention, and extensive scheduled revision (murāja'ah), tailored to each student's capacity.

Sound Recitation

تلاوة متقنة

Practical application of Tajweed rules, correct articulation points, and listening sessions that build clean, fluent, and beautiful reading.

Noble Manners

الأخلاق الحميدة

Fostering good character (akhlāq) and social etiquette (adab), upon the prophetic principle that the Deen is dealings.

About Us

All of our programs are supervised by Shaykh Muhammad Abdelaziz, who sets the curriculum, assesses every student before they begin, and oversees each teacher's work.

Classes are taught by qualified teachers for kids, youth, and adults — brothers and sisters alike — so that every student learns in a setting suited to them.

A full biography of the Shaykh — his studies, teachers, and ijāzāt — is coming soon, in shā' Allāh.
