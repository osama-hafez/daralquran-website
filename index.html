What Your Child Will Gain

Qur'an Memorization

الحفظ
  • New memorization: tailored daily goals based on each student's capability.
  • Recent revision (qarībah): daily reinforcement of newly memorized portions.
  • Long-term revision (ba'īdah): a systematic cycle through previous ajzā' to keep everything strong.

Recitation & Tajweed

التلاوة والتجويد
  • Individual guidance: one-on-one listening to build fluency and remove fear of reading.
  • Daily micro-lessons: fundamental rules such as Nūn Sākinah, Madd, and Makhārij.
  • Love of recitation: instilling appreciation for the melodic beauty of the Qur'an.

Character & Fun

الأخلاق
  • Daily ethics session: manners, Prophetic stories, and a daily Hadith focus.
  • Games and outdoor time during a full midday recess.
  • Congregational prayer: students pray Dhuhr together each day.

Adult Tajweed Classes

Weekend classes for adults who want to perfect their recitation of the Qur'an — studying the classical Tajweed texts and reciting directly to Shaykh Muhammad Abdelaziz.

Drop In on Weekends

There is no fixed enrollment schedule — classes run on weekends and you simply drop in and join. Come when you can, and progress at your own pace.

Learn Together

Lessons are taught in a group setting: the Shaykh walks through the rules of Tajweed with the whole class, verse by verse from the classical poems, with examples from the Qur'an.

Recite One-on-One

Within the group class, recitation is individual — you wait your turn, then recite directly to Shaykh Muhammad and receive personal correction and feedback.

What You Will Study

Students progress through the classical poems of Tajweed and Qirā'āt according to their level. At each level, you will memorize the poem, recite it to the Shaykh, and be tested on your understanding of its rules.

Tuḥfat al-Aṭfāl

تحفة الأطفال

Foundation level. The beloved poem of Sulaymān al-Jamzūrī covering the essentials: the rulings of nūn sākinah and tanwīn, mīm sākinah, and the types of madd.

Al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah

المقدمة الجزرية

Intermediate level. The classic of Imam Ibn al-Jazarī: articulation points (makhārij), letter characteristics (ṣifāt), and the finer rules of precise recitation.

Ash-Shāṭibiyyah

الشاطبية

Advanced level. The masterpiece of Imam ash-Shāṭibī on the seven canonical readings (qirā'āt), for students ready to go beyond a single narration.
