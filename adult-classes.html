Adult Tajweed Classes

Weekend classes for adults who want to perfect their recitation of the Qur'an — from the basics of Tajweed to reciting with precision and beauty, directly under the guidance of Shaykh Muhammad Abdelaziz.

Tuḥfat al-Aṭfāl تحفة الأطفال

The class is a guided walkthrough of Tuḥfat al-Aṭfāl, the beloved classical Tajweed poem of Sulaymān al-Jamzūrī, studied verse by verse in Arabic with English explanation — with reference to The Essence of Mastery in the Tajweed of the Quran by Sheikh Abdul Rahim Muhammad Habib.

Recitation is taught in the narration of Ḥafṣ from Imam 'Āṣim, the reading of the vast majority of the Muslim world.

How the Class Works

Drop In on Weekends

There is no fixed enrollment schedule — classes run on weekends and you simply drop in and join. Come when you can, and progress at your own pace.

Learn Together

Lessons are taught in a group setting: the Shaykh walks through the rules of Tajweed with the whole class, verse by verse from the poem, with examples from the Qur'an.

Recite One-on-One

Within the group class, recitation is individual — you wait your turn, then recite directly to Shaykh Muhammad and receive personal correction and feedback on your recitation.

Fees

The class is offered on a gift (hadiyyah) basis — this is discussed with the Shaykh directly. Please reach out and he will be happy to speak with you.

What You Will Study

The full classical Tajweed curriculum, covered clearly and practically.

Foundations

What Tajweed is and its ruling, the four paces of recitation (taḥqīq, tartīl, tadwīr, ḥadr), and the ways of beginning recitation and joining sūrahs (isti'ādhah & basmalah).

Nūn Sākinah & Tanwīn

The four rulings — iẓhār, idghām, iqlāb, and ikhfā' — with their letters, mnemonics, and examples from the Qur'an.

Mīm Sākinah & Ghunnah

Ikhfā' shafawī, idghām mithlayn, iẓhār shafawī, and the ghunnah of the emphasized nūn and mīm.

The Rules of Lām

Lām qamariyyah and shamsiyyah (the lunar and solar lām), and the lām of verbs and particles.

Madd — Elongation

The letters of madd, every type from the natural two-count madd to the six-count madd lāzim, and the complete tree of rulings.

The Opening Letters

The fourteen ḥurūf muqaṭṭa'ah that open the sūrahs, and how each category is recited.
