What You Can Study

Five tracks. You are placed in the one that fits your level, and move up as you complete each stage.

1. Reading & Memorization

تقوية القراءة والحفظ

Strengthen your recitation or continue your memorization. You recite one-on-one to the teacher and receive personal correction on your articulation and Tajweed.

2. Tuḥfat al-Aṭfāl

متن تحفة الأطفال

Foundation. The poem of Sulaymān al-Jamzūrī: the rulings of nūn sākinah and tanwīn, mīm sākinah, and the types of madd. An ijāzah in the poem itself is available on completion.

3. Al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah

المقدمة الجزرية

Intermediate. The classic of Imam Ibn al-Jazarī: articulation points (makhārij), the characteristics of the letters (ṣifāt), and the finer rules of precise recitation. An ijāzah in the poem itself is available on completion.

4. Introduction to Ash-Shāṭibiyyah

مقدمة في متن الشاطبية

Advanced. The masterpiece of Imam ash-Shāṭibī on the seven canonical readings, covering both al-Uṣūl and al-Farsh. An ijāzah in the poem itself is available on completion.

5. Ijāzah in a Narration

الإجازة برواية

Reciting the entire Qur'an to the Shaykh in a chosen narration — Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim or another — and receiving a connected chain of transmission back to the Prophet ﷺ.

The Ijāzah Program

Two different things are called an ijāzah, and it is worth being clear about which is which.

Ijāzah in a Poem

إجازة في المتن

You memorize the poem — Tuḥfat al-Aṭfāl, Al-Jazariyyah, or Ash-Shāṭibiyyah — recite it to the Shaykh from memory, and are tested on your understanding of its rules. This certifies the text itself.

Ijāzah in a Narration

إجازة في الرواية

You recite the entire Qur'an, cover to cover, to the Shaykh in that narration. Studying a poem is not a substitute for this — the full recitation is what earns the chain.

Studying more than one narration

Students must complete their ijāzah in Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim before beginning any other narration. Once that foundation is in place, any of the other narrations may be taken up.

For sisters

Sisters study with Ustādhah Huda Muhammad Bashir, who teaches the same tracks and is qualified to grant ijāzah to her students.

How the Class Runs

Drop In on Weekends

There is no fixed enrollment schedule — classes run every weekend and you join when you can, progressing at your own pace.

Learn Together

The rules are taught to the whole class, verse by verse from the classical poems, with examples drawn straight from the Qur'an.

Recite One-on-One

Within the group class, recitation is individual — you wait your turn, then recite directly to the teacher and receive personal correction.

How Registration Works

  1. Get in touch. Call, text, or email us to say which track you are interested in.
  2. Assessment with the Shaykh. He listens to your recitation and places you in the track that fits your level.
  3. Pay securely online. After your assessment, pay by card through Stripe — either a recurring monthly subscription or one month at a time, whichever you prefer.
