What Your Child Will Study

Three tracks, and students move between them as they progress.

Qāʿidah Baghdādiyyah

القاعدة البغدادية

The classical primer for students who are just beginning — learning the letters, their shapes and sounds, and how to join them into words correctly from the very start.

Strengthening Reading

تقوية القراءة

For children who can already read but need fluency and accuracy. Reading aloud to the teacher, with correction on articulation and the basic rules of Tajweed.

Memorization

الحفظ

New memorization with daily targets set to each child's capacity, alongside recent and long-term revision so that what has been memorized stays firm.

Continuing on to ijāzah

A student who finishes here and wants to pursue an ijāzah moves up into the adult class, where the classical Tajweed poems and the narrations are studied in full.

Sisters are welcome

Girls are taught in this class alongside the boys' groups, and sisters who continue on to the adult track study with a qualified female teacher who can also grant ijāzah.

How Registration Works

  1. Fill out the registration form. Tell us about your child and their Qur'an background.
  2. Receive a personal call. Shaykh Muhammad will call you directly to answer any questions you may have.
  3. Individual assessment. Every child is assessed before starting, so they are placed in the right track from day one.
  4. Pay securely online. Once you have been assessed, pay by card through Stripe — either a recurring monthly subscription or one month at a time, whichever you prefer.
