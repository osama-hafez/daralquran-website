How to Register

Five simple steps from sign-up to your first class.

  1. Choose your days. Saturday, Sunday, or both — pick whichever fits your schedule.
  2. Fill out the registration form below. It takes just a few minutes and tells us what we need to get started.
  3. Shaykh Muhammad will call you to discuss your level, answer your questions, and schedule your assessment.
  4. Individual assessment. The Shaykh listens to your recitation and places you in the right track for your level.
  5. Pay securely online through Stripe — either a recurring monthly subscription or one month at a time.
